Star Sweep

Clear squares on the minefield and the matching squares on the sky turn over. Numbers count the bombs in the eight squares around them. Hitting a bomb blows up that one square on both grids and leaves a scorch mark on the minefield, and the game carries on.

Your first square is always safe. Right-click a square (or turn on Flag mode) to flag a suspected bomb. Flagged squares can't be opened until you unflag them. Clicking an empty square also opens the squares touching it, up to the "Max per click" limit (9 at most), side neighbours first. It never spreads further than that.
